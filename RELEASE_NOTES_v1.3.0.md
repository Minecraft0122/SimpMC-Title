# SimpMC-Title 1.3.0

## Added

- Public `SimpMCTitleAPI` service registered with Bukkit's `ServicesManager`.
- Read APIs for title definitions, active and owned titles, formatted player display, internal currency balance, and title groups.
- Async entity-scheduler operations for granting, equipping, forcing, taking, and clearing online player titles.
- Async operations for internal currency and player nickname/name-color updates, plus Vault balance reads.
- Custom-title registration and UUID-based title-group management for integrations.
- Integration guide and examples in `API.md`.

## Compatibility

- Paper 1.20.4+
- Folia 1.20.4+
- Java 17+
