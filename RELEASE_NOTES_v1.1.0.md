# SimpMC-Title 1.1.0

## Added

- Player-facing title shop with GUI and `/smtitle buy` support.
- Two purchase methods: Vault-backed server gold and the built-in per-player currency.
- Configurable gold-to-internal-currency exchange with `all` support and a minimum exchange amount.
- Permanent and timed title purchases, including configurable renewal for timed titles.
- Audit logging for purchases, currency exchanges, nickname and color changes, title management, currency administration, reloads and audit views.
- Configurable title content policy for maximum length, allowed characters, banned words and banned regular expressions.
- Balance and ownership PlaceholderAPI values.

## Compatibility

- Paper 1.20.4+
- Java 17+
- Vault and a Vault-compatible economy plugin are optional.
